## 1. Understanding the Oversized Streetwear Silhouette

Modern collegiate streetwear differs from standard department store apparel in three structural proportions:

1. **Dropped Shoulder Seam:** The shoulder line sits 2–3 inches below the natural acromion bone to create an effortless, relaxed downward slouch without rigid shoulder peaks.
2. **Architectural Mockneck:** A structured, high-density collar that stands upright around the throat without suffocating or losing elasticity after laundering.
3. **Snug Elastic Waist Ribbing:** Anchors the garment at the waistline, enabling a modern boxy crop or natural drape over denim.

## 3. Pit-to-Pit vs. Chest Width Guide

To measure your favorite sweatshirt at home:
1. Spread the garment flat on a hard, unyielding surface (table or floor).
2. Smooth out wrinkles from armpit to armpit without stretching the fabric.
3. Place a tape measure 1 inch below the lowest point of the armhole seam across the chest to the opposite side.
4. **True to Size (TTS):** Order your normal unisex size for lookbook volume.
5. **Size Down:** Order one size smaller for tailored silhouettes under structured trench coats or blazers.

## 4. Fabric Density & GSM Analysis

| Fabric Weight | GSM Range | Structural Characteristics | Best Season |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lightweight** | 220 – 280 GSM | Thin drape, fast drying, prone to rib curl | Spring / Indoor |
| **Midweight** | 300 – 380 GSM | Standard athletic fleece, moderate warmth | Fall Layering |
| **Heavyweight (Parke Standard)** | 400 – 480 GSM | Structured collar stand, heavyweight drape, anti-pilling | Fall / Winter / Everyday |

## 5. Garment Care & Anti-Shrink Washing Protocol

High-cotton heavyweight fleece requires gentle thermal care to preserve sizing and loft:

```
[ Wash Inside Out ] ──► [ Cold Water (<30°C) ] ──► [ Gentle / Delicates ] ──► [ Air Dry Flat ]
```

- **Never Tumble Dry Hot:** Heat shrinks cellulose cotton fibers by up to 8% and degrades varsity felt embroidery.
- **Detergent:** Use mild liquid detergents; avoid bleach and aggressive fabric softeners that coat breathable fleece pores.
